# Consensus Challenge

Use the [consensus challenge contract](../../references/consensus-challenge.md) and `../../scripts/consensus_challenge.py` to build an auditable evidence pack before writing a critique. The user does not need to know the skill name. This is distinct from `research-quality-review`: the object is a *cross-house shared thesis*, not the accuracy of one report.

Define the eligible large sell-side coverage universe for this issuer and date, with a documented basis. Gather only reports/recommendations the user is entitled to access or lawful public summaries; do not bypass paywalls, commit proprietary notes or reproduce substantial copyrighted text. Date every house view and reconcile changing recommendations. Group subsidiaries under their parent research house. A buy-rating majority alone does not establish a mainstream theory: match the claim, causal mechanism and horizon. If the denominator or report content is inaccessible, say “convergence among observed notes,” not “the Street consensus.”

First steelman the common case. Then test its load-bearing assumptions against independent primary filings, segment economics, supply-chain and customer data, competitor behavior, accounting/cash conversion, regulatory constraints, estimate revisions, valuation expectations and timing. Prioritize where an assumption is both widely shared and vulnerable to a dated, source-backed falsifier. Search for existing dissent; “not addressed in this reviewed sample” does not mean “nobody spotted it.” Distinguish a verified contradiction from a fragile assumption, a plausible alternative, and an unresolved gap. State the strongest defense of the consensus view and what would disprove the proposed variant.

Output a concise challenge brief: precise mainstream claim and coverage denominator; shared premises; two or three highest-value critiques with source and counterargument; whether each matters for earnings or valuation; dated tests and falsifiers; and confidence/coverage limits. If no thesis passes the gate or no material critique survives, say so. Do not manufacture a contrarian call or investment recommendation.
